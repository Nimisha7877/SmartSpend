# 💳 SmartSpend 

### AI-Powered BNPL Platform for Overspending Prevention & Debt Risk Analysis

SmartSpend AI is a fintech-focused Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) platform designed to help users make smarter financial decisions. Unlike traditional BNPL systems that primarily focus on enabling purchases, SmartSpend AI aims to reduce overspending and prevent debt traps through intelligent risk assessment and financial wellness features.

The platform supports students, salaried professionals, freelancers, and first-time earners by providing personalized financial insights and spending analysis.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Traditional BNPL platforms often encourage spending without adequately helping users understand the financial impact of their decisions. SmartSpend AI focuses on responsible spending by combining BNPL services with AI-driven financial risk assessment.

The goal is to create a platform that not only enables purchases but also promotes financial discipline and informed decision-making.
